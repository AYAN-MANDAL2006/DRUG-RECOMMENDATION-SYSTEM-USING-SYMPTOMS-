2. No Autonomous Diagnosis or Prescription

This tool does NOT state "You have disease X" or "You must take medicine Y". It does not calculate personalized dosage, duration of therapy, or prescribe controlled substances.

3. Emergency Medical Disclaimer

If you are experiencing severe or life-threatening symptoms—such as severe chest pain, sudden difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, uncontrollable bleeding, seizures, or signs of stroke—DO NOT use this website. Call emergency emergency services or visit the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.
